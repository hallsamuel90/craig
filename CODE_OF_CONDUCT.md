# Code of Conduct

Be good to people.

Craig should be a place for curious, constructive conversation. Be respectful,
assume good intent, and criticize ideas or work rather than the person behind
them.

Harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, and sharing someone else's private
information are not welcome.

If something goes wrong, contact hallsamuel90@gmail.com. The maintainer may
remove contributions or restrict participation when needed.
